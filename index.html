Today's task

Classify each image according to its main issue. Then use short phrases to explain who has power, what they decide, and who pays the price. We'll model Boss Tweed together. You may talk with a partner, but write your own responses.

Issue word bank: political corruption · economic inequality · working/living conditions · philanthropy and business practices · nativism. Categories may repeat.

“Pays the price” can mean losing money, safety, opportunity, dignity, political voice, or control over daily life. Describe the image as “According to the cartoonist…” or “According to the illustrator…” rather than treating every claim as established fact.

Individual formative response

Choose two images. What common message do they communicate about power during the Gilded Age? Use one specific visual detail from each to support your answer. Images may express different viewpoints; explain the connection you see.

Discussion bridge: How could people challenge these arrangements of power?
